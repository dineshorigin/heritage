v3.16 device acceptance checklist
1. Open updated game: mode cards and shaded icons, no settings form on first step. Check portrait/landscape and Back/Continue.
2. Play computer: enter only your own name; opponent must be Azhagi (AI), never editable. Test all four AI levels. New mode should begin with cowries. Existing material choices remain selectable in step 3.
3. Pass N Play: only your name input, fixed other-player name; both hint lamps visible and facing their respective players. Only active player's lamp enabled.
4. Connect from TWO devices on v3.16. Enter different own names before/after joining. Check automatic opponent display. Neither may edit the other's name.
5. Ready: host cannot start with zero/one ready player. Both Ready enables host only. Cancel readiness disables Start. Changing shared rules clears readiness on both sides. Guest rule controls disabled. Disconnect/reconnect and re-ready. Test rematch separately after game end.
6. Move in online game: only the active local device should play the turn notice. The waiting device should not. Test sound mute and 1% slider increments; enabled buttons should give a tiny click when sound is enabled.
7. Test light/dark switch on home and Tools, persistence after refresh, and Tamil/English UI including default player names. Custom names should remain unchanged.
8. Inspect outer-edge pit counts, store side counts, painted black pupils and lamps at Fit and normal sizes. Comments should not resize the board.
9. Recheck old features: rules, all six tutorial lessons, daily puzzle, replay, Undo, room chat, fourteen mats, seed materials, result fireworks and saved match recovery.
10. Install/offline: first load online, let cache finish, close/reopen offline. Tutorial/AI/puzzle should work; online room/chat require internet. Close all old tabs and reopen online after each update.
