📖 How to play

This game uses relay-sowing rules. Regional Pallanguzhi traditions may differ. Agree on Pasu and Kasie before starting.

1. Board: Each player owns 7 cups. Every cup starts with 4 or 5 seeds (56 or 70 total), depending on setup. The outer cups store captured seeds and are not part of the sowing path.

2. Your move: Select a nonempty cup from your own row. Pick up all its seeds and sow one into each next cup, counter-clockwise. You may sow into both rows.

3. Keep sowing: When your hand becomes empty, examine the next cup. If it has seeds, pick them up and continue from the following cup.

4. Capture: If that next cup is empty, your turn ends. Capture all seeds in the cup immediately after the empty one, if any. They go to the current player's store.

5. Pasu: Whenever sowing makes a cup contain exactly 4 seeds, its owner immediately collects those 4 seeds into their store, even during an opponent's move.

6. Match end: When either player has no playable cup, the round ends and remaining playable seeds return to their owner. In Single Match, most seeds wins; in Kasie Tournament, remaining seeds refill as many cups as possible, with unfilled cups locked next round. If the same seed allocation repeats three times, the tournament ends as a draw.

7. Smart play: Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert when facing the computer. Tap 💡 Hint on your turn for a suggested cup; the suggestion does not play for you. Tap ↶ Undo to restore the previous human decision (including the computer's reply in AI mode). Undo also works during an animation.

8. Interactive lessons: Open Learn from Match settings or Rules for six bilingual practice lessons, now including the traditional Kasie tournament rules.

9. Save & resume: The game saves after completed moves. Calls, screen locks and app switches pause play and finish any active sowing safely. Tap Resume to continue; use Continue on the home screen after reopening.

10. Online multi-device: Two devices connect by room code through the Cloudflare room server. Both players need internet. The server validates moves and waits for both phones to finish playback.
