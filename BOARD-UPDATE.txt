REFERENCE BOARD EDITION v3.11.0

The interactive game now uses a close reconstruction of the supplied wooden Pallanguzhi board photograph: ivory elephant heads, green/red details, reddish wood, painted central flower, fourteen dark cups and two shallow collection bowls. Cowrie shells use one reusable transparent photographic sprite. No shells are baked into the board. Counts, turn highlights, locked cups, pickup/sowing/capture remain live game elements.

The drawing is an approximation reconstructed from the angled photograph, not a 100% identical copy. The board is scaled to the largest size that fits its allotted stage without distorting its proportions. Landscape gives the largest cups. Static artwork uses cached compressed WebP files; no new graphics framework or game engine. Board and shell artwork together: 311130 bytes (~304 KiB). Maximum 20 visible shells per cup and 30 per store; numeric counts remain exact.
