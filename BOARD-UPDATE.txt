BOARD POLISH v3.11.1

Changes requested:
1. Names and turn/waiting status now live in the upright header. Redundant rotated TOP/BOTTOM and store-name labels are hidden. Store numbers and cup counts remain upright in portrait.
2. Darker wood with a static, subtle epoxy-style reflection. This assumes “epoch” meant epoxy. The existing reference image is reused; no new texture download or graphics dependency.
3. Cowrie sprites enlarged (cup shell width from 28% to 38% of cup width, with bounded sizes).
4. Small handfuls now settle into compact piles near the low central region (50%,58%) instead of spreading to the rim. Larger piles remain bounded; store piles also settle centrally. Counts remain exact. This is a visual arrangement, not a physics engine.
5. Board now fills both dimensions of its available stage. On 393x851 preview it measures 375x672, compared with 257x685 in the previous fixed-aspect layout. The artwork adapts to the stage proportions; it is no longer constrained to its original 8:3 ratio.
6. Warm, dark textured background matches the wood. Removed persistent playable outlines, hint/sowing/capture rings, store outlines, and landing rings. Hint uses a small star; active player status stays in the header. Locked Kasie badges remain visible without an extra circular outline.

Validation on this version:
29 unit/regression tests passed, including bounded shell piles and board stage filling.
Six browser viewports passed: 320x568, 360x640, 393x851, 412x915, 740x360, 1024x768.
All 84 cup centers were unobstructed. Header, board, guidance and footer did not overlap; no horizontal overflow.
At each viewport: artwork loads, upright turn status is inside player cards, inverted row labels are hidden, cup border width is zero and shadow absent, cowrie size check, legal sowing matches rules engine, seed conservation, Hint/Undo, Pause/Resume, language round trip and all five themes passed.
Android source sync passed; no APK built.

The earlier version's eight full local matches and 800 simulations were not rerun for this presentation-only update. Physical phone frame rate, touch ergonomics, real internet multiplayer and native installation remain unverified. No GitHub upload or deployment was performed.
