PALLANGUZHI v3.17.0 — APPEARANCE, IDENTITY & TURN CLOCK

1. ONE LIGHT/DARK SETTING
Home toolbar and gameplay Tools use the same persistent setting, action labels and pressed state. Browser theme colour follows it. Online opponents may choose their own appearance independently; rules/turns are unaffected.

2. DARKER GAMEPLAY
Dark mode dims the actual board texture, ivory edge, mat and resting pieces; it is no longer just a menu colour change. Counts, names and active-player feedback stay readable. Light mode restores normal material brightness. Transitions respect reduced-motion preferences.

3. PASS N PLAY NAMES
Both player names can now be edited in step 2. The second local name is remembered separately from AI/online identities. Computer opponent remains fixed Azhagi (AI); room opponent still comes from the other device. Default names localize; custom names remain as entered.

4. LEFT PUPIL
Measured the left carved eye from a close crop of the actual 1600x600 board texture. Black pupil moved from 8.7%,37.3% to 9.2%,35.7%, centred on the carved iris. Board artwork and cup calibration were not regenerated.

5. TURN CLOCK
Step 3 > Match rules offers Off, 30, 60 or 90 seconds. Default is 30 seconds to CHOOSE a starting cup, not to finish the sowing animation. Final ten seconds have one short tick per displayed second; last three ticks are higher. Mute/volume apply. In online games only the active local player device ticks or triggers timeout. AI thinking does not need a human countdown. Pass N Play uses the shared device sound.
At zero, the first legal cup whose rule simulation finishes is chosen automatically. The normal move handler and online MOVE validation are used. No seeds/scores are removed. If no safely simulated cup can be found, a warning is shown rather than inventing a move.
Clock pauses during sowing, explicit pause, backgrounding, disconnection, synchronization and round summaries. Local help/review overlays hold the clock. Opening the interactive tutorial pauses the match; Resume is available afterward. Online chat/review does not silently pause the opponent's match; use Pause when needed.
Remaining local decision time is restored from stable saved progress and retained on same-turn state synchronization. Opponent countdown snapshots are informational and arrive about once per second. This is a friendly, active-client clock, NOT a server-enforced competitive timer or anti-cheat mechanism. Real network behaviour remains a device test. Both room clients must use v3.17 because timer settings participate in readiness signatures.

6. PLAYER LABELS
Each player name is now the upper line of its compact player strip, with score/turn/time below. In Pass N Play, the top player's whole strip faces that player. In Connect, the top strip remains readable from the device's viewing direction. The board is not automatically flipped for the guest. Hint lamp visibility and ownership are retained.

7. GAME ICON
A new generated walnut/ivory-cowrie/emerald icon appears beside Pallanguzhi on the menu and gameplay header, as browser favicon, touch icon and PWA launcher icon. Android source launcher images have also been replaced. Native source sync passed; no APK built. Installed-launcher icon refresh timing depends on the OS/browser. Small 192px/512px PNGs are used; full generation prompt is in ICON-NOTES.txt.

VALIDATION
